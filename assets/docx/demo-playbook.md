# Demo playbook: Cooper Charter Township

## Why Cooper is the first target

| Signal                        | Evidence (public)                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One overloaded clerk's office | The clerk's published duty list covers agendas, minutes, payroll, HR, the township website, and the phone system |
| Minutes on a public clock     | Draft minutes are posted as PDFs, so residents can see posting times                                             |
| Holiday collision             | The board meets the 2nd Monday, which is Columbus Day (a Michigan legal holiday) every October                   |
| Right size                    | A charter township with a seven-member board, close to Kalamazoo and reachable in person                         |
| Clear decision path           | Clerk (FOIA coordinator and minute-keeper) and Supervisor (agenda)                                               |

Sources: [Clerk responsibilities PDF](https://www.coopertwp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Cooper-Township-Clerk-Responsibilities.pdf),
[Boards and committees](https://www.coopertwp.org/boards-and-committees/),
[FOIA page](https://www.coopertwp.org/freedom-of-information-act/).

## 20-minute walkthrough script

| Minute | Screen        | What to show                                                                          | What to ask                                                              |
| ------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–3    | Monday brief  | Her name, this week's dates, the deadline rail with weekends and holidays             | "How do you keep track of FOIA due dates today?"                         |
| 3–9    | FOIA desk     | The bulk request: legal receipt rule, due date, the one extension, the drafted notice | "How many of these out-of-state bulk requests have you had this year?"   |
| 9–13   | FOIA desk     | The partial grant: exemption flags and the six required elements checklist            | "Who reviews your denials now? Your attorney?"                           |
| 13–16  | Minutes       | Their heading and certification line; the 8-business-day clock                        | "How long does a three-hour meeting take you to write up?"               |
| 16–19  | Resident line | Tap "When are my winter taxes due?" then type something off-script                    | "Which calls take the most of your day?"                                 |
| 19–20  | Close         | Fixed price, one board vote, design-partner terms                                     | "Would it help if I put together a one-page summary for the Supervisor?" |

## Follow-up after the walkthrough

Send the Supervisor a one-page summary within 24 hours: what the clerk saw, the fixed price,
the design-partner terms, and a draft board resolution.
